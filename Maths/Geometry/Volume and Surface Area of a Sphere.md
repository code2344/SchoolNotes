# Volume and Surface Area of a Sphere

For a sphere with radius $r$:

$$V=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$$

$$A=4\pi r^2$$

For $r=5$:

$$V=\frac{4}{3}\pi(5)^3=\frac{500}{3}\pi\approx523.6\text{ units}^3$$

Volume is measured in cubic units, while surface area is measured in square units. Compare this with [[Total Surface Area of a Cone]].
