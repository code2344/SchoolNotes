# Radical Equation

Given:

$$y=\sqrt{(x+2)^2-x^2}$$

$$x+y=23$$

Substitute $y=23-x$ into the first equation:

$$\sqrt{(x+2)^2-x^2}=23-x$$

Simplify the expression under the square root:

$$\sqrt{4x+4}=23-x$$

Squaring both sides gives:

$$4x+4=(23-x)^2$$

Any solutions obtained after squaring must be checked in the original equations to exclude extraneous solutions.
