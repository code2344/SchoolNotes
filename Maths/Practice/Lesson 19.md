## Form refresher

Use **factor form** when the roots or $x$-intercepts are known:

$$y=a(x-r_1)(x-r_2)$$

Use **turning-point form** when the turning point $(h,k)$ is known:

$$y=a(x-h)^2+k$$

Substitute another known point $(x,y)$ to find $a$.
