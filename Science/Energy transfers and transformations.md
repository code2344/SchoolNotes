Work involves applying a force on an object that causes movement across a distance. Energy is measured in units called joules (J). Energy is defined as the capacity to do work.

$$
\begin{alignedat}{3}
\text{Work} &={} & \text{Force} &\times{} & \text{Displacement}\\
\text{W}    &={} & \text{F}     &\times{} & \text{s}
\end{alignedat}
$$
$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
W & = & \text{Work (J or Nm)} \\
F & = & \text{Force (N)} \\
s & = & \text{Displacement (m)}
\end{array}
$$

$$\text{E}_{mechanical}=\text{E}_{kinetic}+\text{E}_{potential}$$
where $\text{E}_{kinetic}$ is calculated by:
$$\text{E}_{k}=\frac{1}{2}\cdot{}\text{m}\cdot{}\text{v}^2$$
$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
\Delta{}\text{E}_{k} & = & \text{kinetic energy (J)} \\
m & = & \text{mass (kg)} \\
v & = & \text{speed (}\text{m/s}\text{)} \\
\end{array}
$$

and $\text{E}_{potential}$ is calculated by:
$$\Delta{}\text{E}_{p}=\text{m}\cdot{}\text{g}\cdot{}\Delta{}\text{h}$$
$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
\Delta{}\text{E}_{p} & = & \text{potential energy (J)} \\
m & = & \text{mass (kg)} \\
g & = & \text{gravity (}\text{9.81m/s}^2\text{)} \\
\Delta\text{h} & = & \text{change in height (m)}
\end{array}
$$

Hard question:
An 850kg rollercoaster has 420kJ of mechanical energy. Determine the speed of the rollercoaster when it is 15m above the ground.

$$\text{E}_{p}=\text{850kg}\cdot{}\text{9.81m/s}^2\cdot{}\text{15}$$
$$\text{E}_{p}=\text{125,077.5J}$$
$$\text{E}_{m} = \text{420000J}$$
$$\text{420000J}-\text{125,077.5J}=\text{294,922.5J}$$
$$\text{E}_{k}=\text{294,922.5J}$$
$$\text{2}\cdot\frac{294,922.5J}{850}=\text{693.9352941176 m/s}^2$$
$$\text{V}=\sqrt{693.9352941176}=\text{26.3426516152 m/s}$$
