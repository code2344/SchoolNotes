# Oxidation Reactions

Oxidation reactions involve a substance gaining oxygen or losing electrons. Many oxidation reactions are exothermic, meaning they release energy.

- Corrosion is a slow oxidation process involving metals.
- Combustion is rapid oxidation that releases energy.

Hydrocarbons contain only carbon and hydrogen, such as methane ($\mathrm{CH_4}$). Complete combustion follows this pattern:

**hydrocarbon + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water**

For methane:

$$\mathrm{CH}_{4(g)} + 2\mathrm{O}_{2(g)} \rightarrow \mathrm{CO}_{2(g)} + 2\mathrm{H_2O}_{(g)}$$

This equation demonstrates [[Conservation of Mass and Chemical Equations|conservation of mass]]. Cellular respiration is discussed in [[Photosynthesis and Respiration]].
