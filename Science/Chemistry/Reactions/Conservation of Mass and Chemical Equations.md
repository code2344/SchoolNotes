# Conservation of Mass and Chemical Equations

Matter is not created or destroyed in a chemical reaction. A balanced equation therefore has the same number of atoms of each [[Chemical Elements|element]] on both sides.

Coefficients placed before chemical formulas show the relative amounts of each reactant and product. Subscripts within a formula must not be changed when balancing an equation because doing so would change the substance.

Examples appear in [[Ionic Formulas]], [[Bases and Neutralisation]] and [[Oxidation Reactions]]. The reusable notation is in [[Chemical Equation]].
