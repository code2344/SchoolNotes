# Atoms

Atoms are the smallest units that retain the chemical properties of [[Chemical Elements|an element]]. They are made of [[Proton|protons]], [[Neutron|neutrons]] and [[Electron|electrons]], collectively introduced in [[Subatomic Particles]].

The number of protons determines which element an atom belongs to. A neutral atom has equal numbers of protons and electrons; an atom that gains or loses electrons becomes an [[Ions|ion]].
