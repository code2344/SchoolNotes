# Hydrochloric Acid and Sodium Hydroxide

Hydrochloric acid and sodium hydroxide undergo the [[Bases and Neutralisation|neutralisation]] reaction:

$$\mathrm{HCl}_{(aq)} + \mathrm{NaOH}_{(aq)} \rightarrow \mathrm{NaCl}_{(aq)} + \mathrm{H_2O}_{(l)}$$

The hydrogen and hydroxide [[Ions|ions]] combine to make water:

$$\mathrm{H^+}_{(aq)} + \mathrm{OH^-}_{(aq)} \rightarrow \mathrm{H_2O}_{(l)}$$
