# Atomic Structure Revision — 21 September 2026

Given fluorine-19:

- [[Proton|Protons]]: 9
- [[Neutron|Neutrons]]: $19 - 9 = 10$
- [[Electron|Electrons]] in a neutral atom: 9

The atomic number gives the number of protons. Subtracting the atomic number from the mass number gives the number of neutrons. A **neutral** atom—not necessarily a stable atom—has equal numbers of protons and electrons.

Fluorine is in period 2, so its electrons occupy two energy levels. The first level contains 2 electrons and the second contains 7. See [[Atoms]], [[Chemical Elements]] and [[Ions]].
