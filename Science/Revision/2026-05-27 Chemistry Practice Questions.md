# Chemistry Practice Questions — 27 May 2026

[[Atoms]] contain [[Subatomic Particles|subatomic particles]]. A dense nucleus contains [[Proton|protons]] with positive charge and [[Neutron|neutrons]] with no charge. Negatively charged [[Electron|electrons]] occupy energy levels around the nucleus.

[[Ions]] are atoms or groups of atoms that have gained or lost electrons. Losing electrons produces a cation; gaining electrons produces an anion.

## Questions

- What types of elements form cations, and where are they found on the periodic table?
- What types of elements form anions, and where are they found on the periodic table?
- What is a polyatomic ion?
- Draw the electron configurations of a nitrogen atom and nitride ion.
- Explain how an ionic compound forms.
