# Science Practice Questions — 3 August 2026

An [[Acids|acid]] produces hydrogen ions in water and usually has a pH below 7. An alkali produces hydroxide ions in water and has a pH above 7. The pH scale is logarithmic. An indicator is a substance that changes colour according to pH.

## Carbon-cycle response

A carbon atom may begin in the atmosphere as carbon dioxide. A plant absorbs the carbon dioxide during [[Photosynthesis and Respiration|photosynthesis]], incorporating the carbon into glucose and other organic molecules.

Continue by tracing how the carbon could move through a food chain and return to the atmosphere through respiration, decomposition or combustion.
