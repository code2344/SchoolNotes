# Photosynthesis and Respiration

All living organisms need energy to grow, repair, move and reproduce. The Sun is the original source of energy for most ecosystems.

Plants, algae and some bacteria use photosynthesis to convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose:

$$6\text{CO}_{2(g)} + 6\text{H}_{2}\text{O}_{(l)} \xrightarrow{\text{light}} \text{C}_{6}\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_{6(aq)} + 6\text{O}_{2(g)}$$

Aerobic cellular respiration releases usable energy from glucose:

$$\text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_{6(aq)} + 6\text{O}_{2(g)} \rightarrow 6\text{CO}_{2(g)} + 6\text{H}_2\text{O}_{(l)} + \text{energy (ATP)}$$

Respiration is an oxidation process related to the reactions described in [[Oxidation Reactions]], but it occurs through controlled reactions inside living cells.
