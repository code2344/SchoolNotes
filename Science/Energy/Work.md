## Formula sheet

### Work

$$W=Fd$$

$$F=\frac{W}{d}\qquad d=\frac{W}{F}$$

- $W$ = work done, in joules ($\text{J}$)
- $F$ = force in the direction of movement, in newtons ($\text{N}$)
- $d$ = distance moved, in metres ($\text{m}$)

### Gravitational potential energy

$$E_p=mgh$$

$$m=\frac{E_p}{gh}\qquad h=\frac{E_p}{mg}$$

- $E_p$ = gravitational potential energy, in joules ($\text{J}$)
- $m$ = mass, in kilograms ($\text{kg}$)
- $g$ = gravitational field strength ($10\text{ N/kg}$ here)
- $h$ = change in height, in metres ($\text{m}$)

### Kinetic energy

$$E_k=\frac{1}{2}mv^2$$

$$m=\frac{2E_k}{v^2}\qquad v=\sqrt{\frac{2E_k}{m}}$$

- $E_k$ = kinetic energy, in joules ($\text{J}$)
- $m$ = mass, in kilograms ($\text{kg}$)
- $v$ = speed, in metres per second ($\text{m/s}$)
