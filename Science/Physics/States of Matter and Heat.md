# States of Matter and Heat

| Property | Solid | Liquid | Gas |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Particle spacing | Very close | Close | Far apart |
| Arrangement | Usually regular | Random | Random |
| Movement | Vibrate around fixed positions | Move past one another | Move freely in all directions |
| Typical particle energy | Low | Moderate | High |

A bathmat usually feels warmer than a tiled floor at the same temperature because it transfers thermal energy away from your feet more slowly. This distinction between thermal transfer and temperature is explained in [[Heat, Temperature and Sound]].
