Given Fluorine, mass 19

Atom:
Protons = 9
Neutrons = 10
Electrons = 9

You can get the protons from the atomic number. You can then get the number of neutrons from the atomic mass, which in this case is 19, so a fluorine atom has 10 neutrons. If an atom is stable, it has the same amount of protons and electrons, so this atom has 9 electrons. 
To draw the diagram, fluorine is in the second row of the periodic table so it has 2 shells. Shell 1 takes a max of 2 electrons before they go to the second shell. the second shell can take up to 8.
