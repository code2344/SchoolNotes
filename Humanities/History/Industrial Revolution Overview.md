# Industrial Revolution Overview

The transition from the ancient to the medieval period involved the decline of the Western Roman Empire, the development of empires in Asia, and the rise of feudalism and serfdom in parts of Europe.


1. Black Death (change in land ownership and mass loss of population)
2. Industrial Revolution (huge technological change)
3. Age of Enlightenment (knowledge, detach from the church, economics and politics)

Between 1750 and 1900, Britain's population increased dramatically.

|            | 1750      | 1850       | 1900       |
| ---------- | --------- | ---------- | ---------- |
| Population | 6 million | 21 million | 37 million |

|                   | Changes in the industrial revolution                                                                |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Economic changes  | Increase in world trade, increase in large scale manufacturing and food production                  |
| Social Changes    | population growth, urbanisation, movement of people from rural to cities/towns, type+nature of work |
| Political Changes | imperialism, increased influence of capitalism                                                      |
