# Separation of Powers

The separation of powers divides government power between three branches:

- **Legislature:** debates and makes laws through [[Houses of Parliament|Parliament]].
- **Executive:** administers laws and manages the government.
- **Judiciary:** interprets and applies laws through the courts.

This separation limits the concentration of power and supports the [[Rule of Law]]. The Australian system does not completely separate the legislature and executive because ministers are members of Parliament.

See also [[The Australian Constitution]] and [[Separation of Powers Revision]].
