# Rule of Law

The rule of law is the principle that everyone is subject to and equal before the law, including those who make and administer it. Laws should be public, understandable and enforceable.

It is a foundation of Australia's democracy and legal system. Clause 5 of the covering clauses of [[The Australian Constitution|the Constitution]] makes the Constitution and valid Commonwealth laws binding throughout Australia.
