# Houses of Parliament

The Australian Parliament is the legislative branch described in [[Separation of Powers]].

| Senate                                           | House of Representatives                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 76 senators                                      | 151 members                                                           |
| 12 senators per state                            | The political party which holds a majority of seats is the government |
| 2 from each territory                            | The leader of the government is the prime minister                    |
| The senate is also known as the house of review. |                                                                       |

Both houses participate in making federal laws under the powers granted by [[The Australian Constitution]].
