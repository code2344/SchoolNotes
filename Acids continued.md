A base is an ionic compound that has a pH greater than 7.
They produce negative hydroxide ions (OH^-^) when dissolved in water. 

eg sodium hydroxide (NaOH)

When a base is dissolved in water it produces negative hydroxide ions and positive sodium ions (Na^+^).

NaOH --H~2~O--> OH^-^ + Na^+^ 


General properties of bases:
- Tastes bitter
- Feels slippery/soapy
- Has a pH greater than 7 
- Cleaning products are often bases

common ones:
- baking soda (NaHCO~3~)
- potassium hydroxide (KOH)
- ammonia (NH~3~)

Acid Base reactions are also known as neutralisation reactions. The products are salt + water.

Hydrochloric acid + magnesium hydroxide =

2HCl~(aq)~ + Mg(OH)~2(aq)~ -> MgCL~2(aq)~ + H~2~O~(l)~
