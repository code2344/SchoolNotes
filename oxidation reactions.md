chemical reactions where oxygen is one of the reactants are examples of oxidation reactions. 
oxidation reactions are exothermic,which means they release energy.
oxidation reactions can involve metals or non-metals reacting with oxygen.
corrosion reactions are an example of slow oxidation reactions that involve metals.
combustion reactions are an example of fast oxidation reactions that usually involve non metals or reactive metals.
